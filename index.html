Idlarium

Idlarium is a cozy low poly 3D idle game you play right in your browser. Build a collection of water creatures across eight living diorama habitats: a freshwater swamp, a tiny tide pool, a coral reef, the Amazon River, the open ocean, a kelp forest, the deep sea and polar waters.

Your creatures make pearls, even while you're away. Spend them on new species, decorations and bigger habitats. Befriend rare visitors like whales, sea otters and orcas, hatch eggs that sometimes bring rare color variants, and fill your codex with real facts about every animal.

It's made to be relaxing: nothing ever dies, and forgetting to feed only slows things down a little. Idlarium is free, needs no download or account, and works on phones and desktops.
